# Problem Statement

<!-- Students in university and college goes through difficulty finding part-time jobs suitable for them because of their academic schedules, skills, age requirements, location and available working hours. The existing job platforms may provide many vacancies but do not effectively filter jobs based on individual students constraints. This result in students applying for unsuitable jobs, experiencing conflicts between work and study, travel excessive distances or work excessive and unsuitable hours. FairWork AI addresses this problem by intelligently filtering and matching part-time job opportunities according to each student's needs and limitations. -->

## Working project title

<!-- FairWork AI: Part-Time Job Matching for Students -->


## Target users

<!-- Students seeking part-time employment opportunities during their university studies so that it fits into their academic schedules, skills, age requirements, location and working-hour limitations. -->


## Evidence

<!-- This is because students find difficulty finding suitable part-time jobs because of requirements, working hours, distance, and shift schedules that would conflict with their studies, health and personal limitations. -->


## Urgency and social value

<!-- The problem is urgent because students increasingly rely on part-time employment to support their living expenses, education costs and personal financial needs while studying. FairWork AI provides social value by promoting safe, suitable and fair access to part-time employment for students. By considering factors such as class schedules, skills, age requirements, distance and working-hour limits, the system helps students find jobs that are more compatible with their education and well-being. -->


## SDG alignment

<!-- SDG 8, Decent Work and Economic Growth because it promotes access to suitable, safe and fair employment opportunities for students in Malaysia. -->
